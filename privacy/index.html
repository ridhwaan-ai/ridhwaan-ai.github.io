This policy covers the "Ridhwaan Page Poster" app and the small tools run by Ridhwaan Saib (Aurum-Vertex (Pty) Ltd, trading as Stick Figure Files and Ridhwaan AI, South Africa).

What the app does

The app only publishes our own videos and pictures to Facebook Pages that we own and manage ourselves (Stick Figure Files and rs.sai.books). It does not log in other people, and it is not offered to the public.

What data we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal data of Facebook users through this app. It reads only the basic details of our own Pages (name, ID and a publishing permission) so that it can post to them.

Shop customers

If you buy or download something from our Gumroad shop, Gumroad processes the payment and shares your email address with us so we can deliver your product. We never sell it. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Deleting your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email saibridhwaan199@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

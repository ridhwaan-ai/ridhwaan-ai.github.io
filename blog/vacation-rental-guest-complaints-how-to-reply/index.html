How to Reply to a Vacation Rental Guest Complaint (With Examples)

A guest complaint feels personal, but it is also one of the best chances you have to protect your rating. Guests rarely expect perfection. They expect to be heard and to see the problem handled. Here is a five-step formula you can use for private messages and for public review replies.

The five-step reply formula

  1. Acknowledge it. Name the problem in plain words so they know you read it.
  2. Thank them. For telling you, not for complaining.
  3. Say what you will do. One concrete action and a time.
  4. Do not make excuses. Explain only if it helps them understand, never to shift blame.
  5. Follow up. Check back when it is fixed.

Example 1: the hot water stopped

"Thank you for letting me know, and I am sorry the hot water stopped during your stay. I am sending the plumber this afternoon and I will message you at 4 pm with an update. In the meantime, the [gym/neighbour/option] is a short walk away if you need a quick shower."

Notice what it does: acknowledges, apologises once, gives a time and a stopgap.

Example 2: the place was not as clean as expected

"I am sorry the bathroom was not up to standard when you arrived. That is not how I want guests to find it. My cleaner will return today between [times] to fix it, or I can arrange a partial refund if you prefer. Which would you like?"

Offering a choice gives the guest control, which often defuses the situation.

Example 3: a public reply to a bad review

"Thank you for your feedback. I am sorry the noise from the street disturbed your sleep. I have since added a note to the listing and provided earplugs in the bedroom. I hope we can welcome you again."

Keep public replies short, factual and kind. You are writing for future guests reading the page, not for the reviewer. Never argue, never reveal private details.

What not to do

  • Do not reply while angry. Wait an hour.
  • Do not accuse the guest of lying, even if you disagree.
  • Do not offer a reward in exchange for changing a review. It breaks platform rules.
  • Do not paste guest names, phone numbers or addresses into public replies or into an AI tool.

Use patterns, not one-offs

If three guests mention the same thing, it is not a complaint, it is a to-do. Keep a simple log of complaints and the fix.

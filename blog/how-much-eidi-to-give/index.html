How Much Eidi Should You Give? A Practical Guide and Budget

Eidi, the money or gift given to children (and sometimes younger relatives) on Eid, is a lovely tradition and a surprisingly large expense. Give a small amount to ten children and the total adds up fast. There is no fixed amount, so the best approach is to decide a figure that fits your budget and give it with a smile.

Step 1: Decide your total, not the per-child amount

Most people choose a per-child amount first and are shocked by the total. Reverse it. Decide the total you can comfortably give across Eid, then divide by the number of children you want to give to. Step 2: Count who you give to

Write a list: your own children, nieces and nephews, neighbours' children, young relatives. Many families give to children up to a certain age, or to everyone until they start earning. Choose a rule and keep it, so nobody feels singled out and you do not have to decide on the spot.

Step 3: Choose amounts by age band

A simple scheme works well, with figures that suit your own budget and currency:

  • Toddlers: a small token amount or a small gift.
  • Primary school age: a modest amount they can spend or save.
  • Teens: a slightly larger amount, or a contribution to something they are saving for.

Example with made-up numbers: 8 children x an average of 10 = 80 total. If that is too high, lower the average to 6 and the total becomes 48.

Ideas beyond cash

  • A small gift plus a smaller amount of cash.
  • A savings contribution: put part of the eidi in a jar and let the child choose a goal.
  • A charity share: encourage each child to give a portion of their eidi as sadaqah.
  • An experience, such as a trip to the park or a meal out.

Teach children to handle it

Eidi is a first money lesson. Ask children to split it into three parts: spend, save, give. A simple chart helps. Keep it simple and fair

  • Use the same amount for children of the same age band.
  • Prepare in advance: new notes in envelopes can be set aside weeks early.
  • Do not borrow for eidi. Giving is best done from what you have.
  • Write your plan down so you can look back next year.

Plan the rest of Eid too

Eidi is one line in the Eid budget. Add clothes, food for guests, gifts, travel and charity, then check you are within your total.

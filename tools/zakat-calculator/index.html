How this calculator works

Total qualifying wealth = cash + gold and silver at today's price + shares + business stock + money owed to you that you expect to get back. Subtract debts due now. If the result is at or above the nisab, zakat is 2.5% of it. Below nisab, no zakat is due on that amount.

Things to keep in mind

  • Zakat is due after your wealth has stayed at or above nisab for one full lunar year (hawl). Many people pick one date each year, often in Ramadan.
  • Your home, car and everyday items are generally not zakatable. Jewellery rulings differ between schools, so ask a scholar.
  • Pensions, property held for rent or sale, and loans you may not recover have specific rulings. This tool does not decide them.
  • Pay zakat to eligible recipients, and keep a record. A charity log helps.

This is a simple estimator, not a religious ruling or financial advice. For your own situation, check with a qualified scholar or your local zakat authority.

How the fee is worked out

Cost per turnover = cleaning hours × hourly rate × number of cleaners + laundry + supplies + restock extras. A fee that covers that cost means guests pay for the work their stay creates, and your nightly rate stays your real profit.

Tips for setting a fair cleaning fee

  • Guests compare the total price, not just the nightly rate. A very high cleaning fee on a one-night stay puts people off, so shorter stays often need a lower fee or a higher nightly rate.
  • Check your local area: look at five similar listings and compare their total price for a typical stay.
  • Be honest about what the fee covers and keep the cleaning checklist visible in your listing and welcome book.
  • Track your real cleaning time for a month, then update the numbers here.
  • Check your platform's current fee rules, as they change.

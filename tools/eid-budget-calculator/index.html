How to use this budget

Start with the total you can spend without borrowing. Fill in the areas that matter most to your family first, charity included, then trim the rest until nothing is red. If you have a few months before Eid, divide the total by the number of weeks left to see what to set aside weekly.

Tips to keep Eid affordable

  • Set the eidi amount per child first, then multiply by the number of children. It is the item that surprises people most.
  • Buy clothes early, or reuse and swap outfits within the family.
  • Share the hosting: ask each guest to bring one dish.
  • Pay zakat al-fitr before the Eid prayer; check the amount per person with your local mosque or charity.

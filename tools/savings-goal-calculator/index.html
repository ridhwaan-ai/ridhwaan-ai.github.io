How the calculation works

Remaining = target minus what you have saved. Monthly amount = remaining divided by the months to your deadline. Weekly amount = remaining divided by the weeks. If you enter your own monthly amount instead, the tool tells you the month you will reach the goal.

Tips to reach a savings goal

  • Pay yourself first: move the money the day you are paid, before you can spend it.
  • Give each goal a name and a number. Specific goals are easier to keep than "save more".
  • Build a starter emergency fund first, then work towards three to six months of essential costs.
  • Put windfalls, such as a refund or bonus, straight towards the goal.
  • Review monthly. If you fall behind, extend the deadline rather than quitting.
  • Keep savings in a safe, ordinary account. Avoid anything promising guaranteed high returns.

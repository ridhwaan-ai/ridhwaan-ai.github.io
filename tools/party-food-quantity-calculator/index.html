How it works

The calculator uses common catering rules of thumb per adult, counts a child as about half a portion, and multiplies by your appetite setting. Buffets and potlucks are scaled up a little because guests sample several dishes; appetizer-only parties assume about 10 to 12 pieces per person for a two-hour event.

Tips to avoid running out (or drowning in leftovers)

  • Add 10% extra for gatherings over 20 people, and 1 to 2 extra portions of the main dish for surprise guests.
  • Plan more variety rather than more of one dish: guests take small amounts of many things.
  • Ask about dietary needs when you invite: halaal, vegetarian, allergies. Label dishes clearly.
  • Have containers ready so guests can take leftovers home and nothing is wasted.
  • Cook what you can the day before. See our Ramadan preparation checklist for a make-ahead approach that works for any big meal.

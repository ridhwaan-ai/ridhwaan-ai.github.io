How it works

Tip = bill x tip%. Total = bill + tip. Each share = total divided by the number of people. In the uneven split, each person pays their own order plus the same percentage tip on it.

Tipping notes

  • Customs differ by country. In the US, 15 to 20% is common in restaurants. In many other countries a service charge is already included or tipping is small.
  • Check the receipt first. If a service charge is already added, you may not need another tip.
  • Agree on the split before ordering, especially for shared dishes, so nobody is surprised later.
  • For shared holiday rentals, write down who paid what in one place and settle up once.

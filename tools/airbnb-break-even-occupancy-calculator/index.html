How it works

Each night you book earns your nightly rate. Each stay also earns the cleaning fee but costs you cleaning and supplies. The calculator turns that into a profit per night, then divides your fixed monthly costs by it to find the break-even number of nights. Divide by the days available and you get the break-even occupancy rate.

What is a good occupancy rate?

It depends on your city and season, so check your own booking history and similar listings nearby rather than trusting a national average. If your break-even occupancy is above what comparable listings reach, lower your costs or raise your rate before you list. If it is far below, you have a comfortable margin.

Ways to lower your break-even point

  • Set a minimum stay of two or three nights to cut cleaning turnovers.
  • Charge a cleaning fee that honestly covers the real cost of turnover.
  • Price higher on weekends and holidays. See how to price your Airbnb per night.
  • Reduce questions and damage with clear house rules and a printed guest guide.
